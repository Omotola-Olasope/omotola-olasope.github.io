Commodities, LNG and Freight Analytics

Interactive LNG Arbitrage and Netback Pricing App

An interactive LNG arbitrage app that prices cargoes from a selected origin to 40+ destinations, calculating netback per MMBtu across gas, freight, fuel, port, carbon, financing and FX costs.

What it does: ranks every destination on a single compute, then lets you interrogate the result through route comparison, spread matrices, forward curves, netback term structure, carbon detail and freight breakeven views.

Value add: one click destination ranking, fully transparent cost attribution so every dollar of the netback is traceable, replaces the manual spreadsheets desks maintain by hand, and enables live what if scenario testing without disturbing the base case.

Built for: LNG traders and analysts, commercial and origination teams, shipping and chartering desks, and market analysts.

  • Freight Economics
  • Scenario Modelling
  • Geospatial

Sales Analytics and Coverage Intelligence

Campaign Coverage Dashboard: Turning Raw Call Notes Into Client Coverage

A dashboard for tracking sales campaigns. Each campaign carries a list of clients the team is meant to reach. The app takes the raw call and visit notes our reps log every day, matches them against those client lists, and shows how much of each campaign has actually been covered.

What it answers: who has been contacted and who has not, how they were reached (call, in person or virtual), and by which rep. Coverage is broken down into complete, in progress and never touched, with activity measured across the campaign window.

Why it matters: it turns tens of thousands of scattered, unstructured notes into a clear picture of progress, so team leaders can see the gaps while there is still time to act on them rather than after the campaign closes.

  • Entity Matching
  • Text Processing

Commodities & Derivatives Analytics

Commodities and Commitment of Traders (COT) Analytics Platform on Bloomberg BQuant

I built a comprehensive multi tab analytical dashboard on Bloomberg's BQuant platform that transforms CFTC Commitments of Traders data into actionable market intelligence for commodity traders and portfolio managers.

Market Positioning & Sentiment: Multi-commodity COT snapshot with Z-score extremes, long/short ratios, and trader category breakdowns (Commercial, Managed Money, Producers) with color-coded statistical significance.

Futures Curve Analysis: Interactive term structure dashboard featuring normalized forward curves, calendar spreads, roll yield calculations, and automated regime classification (Contango/Backwardation with intensity scoring).

Advanced Analytics: Market regime detection via Hurst exponents, volatility analysis (IV vs RV), position dynamics tracking, 5-year seasonality patterns, cross-commodity correlations, and notional value exposure analysis.

  • Python
  • CFTC Data
  • Statistical Analysis
